**Three new species and a new genus of chrysilline jumping spiders (Araneae: Salticidae) from tea-growing regions in China.** *Zootaxa.* https://doi.org/10.11646/zootaxa.5890.2.4

#### **Abstract:**

A new chrysilline genus, *Yucattus* Yang, Ni & Zhang, gen. nov., is established for *Y. maofeng* Yang, Ni & Zhang, spec. nov. (♂), discovered in Huangshan City, China. The genus *Madhyattus* is transferred from the tribe Hasariini to Chrysillini, with the addition of a new species, *Madhyattus bohea* Yang, Li & Zhang, spec. nov. (♂ ♀), from Wuyishan City, China. Furthermore, a new species, *Siler puer* Yang, Ni & Zhang, spec. nov. (♂ ♀), is described from Pu'er City and surrounding regions, China.
