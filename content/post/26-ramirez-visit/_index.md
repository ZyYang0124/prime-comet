On November 10, 2026, Dr. Martín Ramírez — President of the International Society of Arachnology and Senior Researcher at Argentina's CONICET — visited the Arachnology Laboratory of Hebei University for an academic exchange, to our great honor and delight.

![](/images/2026-ramirez-visit/2.jpg)

Accompanied by lab members, Dr. Ramírez toured the Hebei University Museum, where he expressed appreciation for the rich arachnid collections.

![](/images/2026-ramirez-visit/3.jpg)

Dr. Ramírez also engaged in in-depth, on-site discussions with the team, covering topics ranging from spider genitalia to phylogenetics.

Dr. Ramírez shared his group's latest findings on the biogeography, diversity, genital morphology, and silk biology of various spider lineages. Dr. Zhaoyi Li then provided an update on the laboratory's ongoing research on the diversification of RTA-clade spiders and the phylogeny of Gnaphosidae.

![](/images/2026-ramirez-visit/4.jpg) ![](/images/2026-ramirez-visit/5.jpg)

![](/images/2026-ramirez-visit/6.jpg)

This visit tightened the academic ties between Hebei University and a world-leading spider systematics group, opening new avenues for international collaboration in spider systematics and evolution.
