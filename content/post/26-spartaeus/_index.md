#### **Abstract:**

Two new species of *Spartaeus* Thorell, 1891 from southern China are described: *S. yingdeensis* sp. nov. (♂♀) from Guangdong Province and *S. rotiscutus* sp. nov. (♂♀) from Yunnan Province. Diagnostic illustrations and photographs are provided.
